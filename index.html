Flash ESP32-C5

Connect the board with a data-capable USB cable. Click the button, select the serial port, and keep the cable connected until the installer reports success.

If the board is not detected, enter its bootloader mode and try again. This installer erases and writes the complete 4 MB firmware image.

BW16 firmware

The BW16 uses the Realtek RTL8720DN UART bootloader, not the ESP32 protocol. Connect the board with a data-capable USB cable. The installer enters download mode, erases the required flash sectors, writes the fixed firmware, and restarts the board.
